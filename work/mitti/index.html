Mitti

Building a café’s events programme from the ground up.

Role
Events Manager
Organisation
Mitti Lifestyle Store & Café
Discipline

The role

Built Mitti’s experiential programming from the ground up, including the flagship initiative Women of Ahmedabad.

The approach

Conceptualized and hosted 4-5 events a month across formats: workshops, raves, screenings, lectures, community brunches. Handled artist management, outreach, and session structuring independently; oversaw execution on the day.

What came of it

Positioned Mitti as a recognized community space in Ahmedabad; sharpened leadership, communication, and adaptability through hands-on curation.
