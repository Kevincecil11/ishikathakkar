Women of Ahmedabad

Open conversations between women from every industry in the city.

Role
Founder, curator & moderator
Organisation

Discipline
Community & Events

The idea

A community initiative bringing together women from every industry in the city. Four editions to date.

The approach

Independent speaker identification, outreach, and session structuring, followed by moderating each conversation. Sessions brought together women from unrelated fields for open conversation.

What came of it

Lasting professional connections between attendees and growing local recognition, including newspaper coverage.
