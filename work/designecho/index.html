DesignEcho

Repositioning a writer as an editorial strategist.

Role
Brand strategy & creative direction
Organisation
DesignEcho Communications, a writing service based in Ahmedabad
Discipline
Brand & Marketing

The brief

To develop brand guidelines that communicate a premium, luxury look and elevate the founder’s positioning from writer to editorial strategist.

The approach

A detailed questionnaire covering goals, service details, styling preferences, and references, followed by logo development and full brand positioning.

What came of it

A premium, cohesive brand identity that repositioned DesignEcho as an editorial strategy consultancy rather than a freelance writing service.

  • Logo development
  • Brand positioning
