AboutIshika Thakkar

I think in stories before I think in strategy.

Give me a brand, a fest, a room full of five-year-olds, or a stage, and I’m doing the same thing every time: looking for what’s actually underneath it, not settling for the obvious read.

I don’t wait around for permission or certainty. I move, and I figure it out as I go, whether that means chasing down a sponsor who’s already said no once, calming a room of children through their first annual day, or standing on a stage I wrote myself with no idea if the audience would laugh in the right places.

What ties all of it together isn’t a job title. It’s that people keep handing me the thing they can’t afford to get wrong, and trusting that once it’s mine, it’s handled. I’ve run departments, not just projects. I’ve built brands, not just posts.

And none of it happened alone. I’d rather build something with the right people around me than take credit for doing it by myself, because the work is always better that way.
