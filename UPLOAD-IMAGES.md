# Kevin's image upload shortcut

Use branch **version-2**.

- Photos: [open photos folder](assets/img/photos/)
- Logos: [open logos folder](assets/img/logos/)

## Upload

1. Open the right folder above.
2. Click **Add file > Upload files**.
3. Drag the original files in and click **Commit changes**.
4. Tell Brain, for example: `Connect stax-cover.jpg to Stax Living and use the other stax files in its gallery.` Include your exact captions and image credits.

Photos are grouped by filename prefix so you only have TWO folders to manage, not dozens. Naming examples are inside each folder. Original images are best; SVG or transparent PNG logos are preferred.

This is a safe upload inbox: files are stored in the repo but are not automatically used in a live section. Brain will connect the right images to the right places after you identify them. Keep the live form and content unchanged unless explicitly requested.
